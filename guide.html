How to use Soma

A calm planner for brains that get overwhelmed by everything at once. This guide shows how Soma fits together, a simple daily and weekly routine, and ready-made setups for common kinds of work.

If you only read one thing: press N (or tap +) to get things out of your head, pick up to 3 for Today, then press Start on one of them. If starting feels impossible, choose Just 5 min. That's the whole method; everything else is optional.

1. The idea in one minute

Soma organizes work in four sizes, from big to small:

LevelWhat it isExample
ProjectAn area or big goal you'll work on for weeks or months.Thesis · Client: Acme · Job search
MilestoneA checkpoint inside a project. Finishing one should feel like progress."Draft chapter 2" · "Website live"
Step (task)A concrete thing to do. Starts with a verb."Outline the methods section"
Focus blockOne timed session of work on a step: 90 minutes by default (you can change it).A step estimated at 2 blocks takes about two sessions.

You plan in blocks, not hours. Blocks are easier to count, easier to protect, and they make progress visible: every finished block earns points and fills a square on your project's Board.

You don't need to plan everything up front. A project with one milestone called "Tasks" is perfectly fine. Add structure only when it helps.

2. Set up in 5 minutes

  1. Brain dump. Press N and paste or type everything on your mind, one item per line. Don't sort it yet. Things that aren't tasks can go in Idea or Admin (switch the type at the top of Quick Add).
  2. Make 1–3 projects. In Quick Add, choose Project and type a name; you'll go straight to adding its first tasks. Fewer projects is better. You can archive the example projects any time.
  3. Set your block length. More → Settings. 90 minutes is the default; many people start with 45 or 60 and a 25-minute warm-up.
  4. Pick a weekly goal. On Home, the This week panel shows your block goal (10 by default, which is 2 a day on weekdays). Start lower than you think. You can always beat it.
  5. Choose your rewards. More → Rewards. Pick real, small treats for each points tier.
  6. Install the app and back up. See Your data. Two minutes now can save you a lot later.

3. Your daily routine

Morning (2 minutes)

  • Open Home. Look at Today: anything you didn't finish yesterday is already there.
  • Pick up to 3 things. Use the suggestions, the Due soon list, or Add to Today from any task. Three finished beats eight started.
  • Anything Slipped? Tap Tomorrow or Next week to give it a new date. No guilt: dates move.

During the day

  • Press ▶ on a Today item to open the focus timer, then Start. You can switch tabs; the timer keeps going and shows in the tab title.
  • Thought pops up mid-block? Press N. During a block, Quick Add files it under Ideas so you can get straight back to work.
  • You'll hear a soft chime 5 minutes before the end: start wrapping up.
  • When the block ends, write one line in "Where did you leave off?" and start a break. Next time you open that task, the note is waiting for you.
  • Finished a task? Tap its round check. Finishing the last task in a milestone completes it.

End of the day

  • Check This week at a glance on Home. Showing up counts more than the number.
  • Done your planned blocks? Stop. Rest is productive too.

4. Your weekly routine (Sunday or Monday, 10 minutes)

  1. Review. Soma offers a short review on Sundays: what got done, what stalled, what to drop.
  2. Plan blocks. In the This week panel, give blocks to 1–2 projects with + and −, then Approve plan. Unfinished blocks roll over automatically.
  3. Optional: plan with AI. Press Plan my week with AI on Home. It copies a prompt with your projects, deadlines and capacity; paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat and pick what feels right from its suggestions. Soma itself never sends your data anywhere.
  4. Look at your Board. Each project's board shows how far along the road you are. It's a good place to notice which project needs love.

6. Setups for different kinds of work

Each setup below is a starting point. Paste the example into Quick Add (pick the project first, or use #project tags) and adjust from there.

Thesis, dissertation or a big paper

Structure
  • One project per thesis (or per paper). Milestones are chapters or paper stages: Outline → Draft → Figures → Advisor feedback → Revise → Submit.
  • Give the milestone a due date for the next meeting with your advisor or committee; the project page then shows the pace you need (blocks per week).
Example paste 
Outline chapter 3 ~1
Draft the results section ~3 !
Make figure 3.2 ~2
Send draft to advisor @fri
Tips
  • Protect 2 writing blocks a day, ideally your sharpest hours. Admin and email go in the Admin tab.
  • Always leave a Pick up here note. Writing restarts are where whole days disappear.
  • "Write chapter 3" is not a task. Use Break it down with AI until each step fits in 1–3 blocks.

Research and lab work (experiments with waiting time)

Structure
  • One project per study or aim; one milestone per experiment or analysis.
  • Make waiting steps visible ("Incubate overnight", "Sequencing run, ~3 days") and give them due dates, so the next step shows up in Due soon exactly when it's ready.
Example paste 
Order reagents for batch 2 @mon
Prepare samples ~2
Run imaging ~3
Analyse batch 2 ~2 @nextweek
Write up results ~2
Tips
  • Count lab days in blocks too. A 3-hour protocol is 2 blocks; that keeps your weekly goal honest.
  • Use the Board as a lab-meeting progress picture: what's done, what's next.

Students: courses, assignments and exams

Structure
  • One project per course this term. Milestones are assignments and exams, each with its due date.
  • Archive each course project at the end of term; nothing is lost.
Example paste 
Read chapter 4 ~1
Problem set 3 ~2 @thu
Make flashcards for midterm ~1
Practice exam 1 ~2 !
Tips
  • Shorter blocks work well for studying: try 45-minute blocks and 25-minute warm-ups in Settings.
  • Start exam prep milestones 2 weeks out; the pace label tells you if you're behind before it's a crisis.

Freelancers and client work

Structure
  • One project per client. Milestones are deliverables or phases: Discovery → First draft → Revisions → Handoff.
  • Invoices, follow-ups and quotes go in Admin (switch Quick Add to Admin), not in client projects.
  • Working on several clients at once? Add #clientname to a line and it goes to that client's project.
Example paste 
Kickoff notes ~1
Homepage design v1 ~3 @fri
Collect feedback from the client @mon
Tips
  • Your weekly block plan is your capacity plan. If the blocks don't fit, the deadline needs to move: say so early.
  • Block estimates become your pricing data. After a few projects you'll know what "a homepage" really costs you.

Job search

Structure
  • One project, "Job search". Milestones: Materials ready → Applications (this week) → Interview prep. Start a fresh "Applications" milestone each week.
Example paste 
Update CV for product roles ~2
Write a cover letter template ~1
Apply: 3 roles from my list ~2
Follow up with Sam about the referral @tomorrow
Tips
  • Set a small, steady weekly goal (5 blocks). Job searching is a marathon; forgiving streaks and rest days are there for a reason.
  • Pick one reward tier just for this project. Rejections are part of the process, not a verdict.

Side project or small business launch

Structure
  • See the example project Launch a side project: check the idea, then ship the smallest useful version, then tell people.
  • Every "what if we also…" goes in Ideas, not the current milestone.
Example paste 
Write a one-paragraph pitch ~1
Talk to 5 potential customers ~3
Build the smallest useful version ~4
Make a simple landing page ~2

Creative work: a book, art, music

Structure
  • One project per piece. Milestones are chapters, songs or pieces; steps are sessions ("Draft scene 4", "Mix the bridge").
Tips
  • Use warm-up blocks to start creative sessions; you can always keep going.
  • Watching the Board fill up is great motivation on long projects with no outside deadline.

Home and life admin: moving, taxes, health

Structure
  • One "Life" project with milestones for the big things ("Move flat", "Taxes 2026"). Quick chores go in Admin.
Example paste 
Find last year's tax documents ~1
Book dentist appointment @tomorrow
Compare 3 moving companies ~1
Change address with the bank
Tips
  • Dreaded tasks are usually just vague. Open it and press Break it down with AI, then do only the first step, with Just 5 min if needed.

Learning a new skill

Structure
  • See the example project Learn a new skill: one resource, its modules as milestones, then a small practice project.
Tips
  • Questions that pop up during a block go in Ideas, so they don't derail the session.

7. When you're stuck

  • Can't start? Choose Just 5 min. When it ends you can stop (it still counts) or keep going, and those 5 minutes count toward the block.
  • The task feels huge or foggy? Break it down with AI, or ask yourself "what's the very first physical action?" and add that as its own step.
  • Overwhelmed by the list? Stay on Home and look only at Today. Everything else can wait.
  • Lost a few days? That's normal. Your best streak is remembered, your rewards stay earned, and slipped dates are one tap away from a fresh start.
  • Bored of the task? Switch to a different project for one block, then come back. Variety is fuel, not failure.
  • Working too long without a break? Use the break timer after each block. Your daily max (Settings) is there to stop hyperfocus burnout.

8. Your data, backups and devices

Soma has no account and no server. Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. That's what makes it private, and it's also why backups matter.

  • Install it as an app. Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar. Safari on Mac: File → Add to Dock. iPhone or iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: menu → Install app. Installed apps keep their data more reliably.
  • Back up. More → Settings → Export JSON saves a file with everything. Soma reminds you once a week.
  • Move to another device by exporting on the old one and choosing Import JSON on the new one.
  • Clearing your browser's site data erases Soma's data on that device. Keep a recent backup.
  • Notifications not showing? Settings → "Notify me when a block ends" shows the status. If it says blocked, allow notifications for this site in your browser's settings.
  • Want to start over? Settings → Reset all data (export a backup first).
