Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open {{productName}} in your browser and start. If you buy Pro, you paste a license key once on each device.

Where are my plans stored?

On your device, in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. That keeps them private, and it's also why {{productName}} reminds you to save a backup file now and then.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit it works without a connection, and you can install it so it opens like a normal app.

Which devices does it work on?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android. To move between devices, export a backup on one and import it on the other.

What happens if I clear my browser?

Clearing a browser's site data erases what's stored there, so keep a recent backup (Settings → Export). Installing {{productName}} as an app also keeps your data more reliably on iPhone and Mac.

Is this a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that includes all version 1 updates.

Can I use it for a lab, a class or a team?

Yes. Each person uses their own copy. For group licenses, email {{supportEmail}}.

Is {{productName}} a treatment for ADHD?

No. It's a planning tool designed to be ADHD-friendly. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.
